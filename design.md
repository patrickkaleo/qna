# qna.pacquos.com

A fun place for Pestalozzi Academy pupils, teachers, and alumni to work on mathematics together. People ask, answer, vote, and talk on the same feed. The first feed is Mathematics. Everything else on a question is a label used to filter that feed.

Conversation stays on the feed. There are no private messages.

## People

- **Pupil** — currently at Pestalozzi. Asks, votes, comments, and accepts the answer that helped.
- **Teacher** — teaches at Pestalozzi. Answers, comments, pins the question of the week, and confirms who is a pupil. A teacher badge comes from the school. Points do not grant it.
- **Alumnus** — finished at Pestalozzi. Answers and comments. A verified badge comes from being an alumnus of the school. Points do not grant that badge.

## How someone gets in

A pupil enters their name, grade, curriculum, and a class code for their mathematics class. The code shows they were invited. The teacher’s class list is what confirms them. A confirmed pupil can ask, vote, earn points, and appear on the board. A person with the code who is not on the list can read. Their questions stay unmarked and earn nothing. A leaked code is reset by the teacher. People already confirmed stay confirmed.

A school email on the Pestalozzi domain confirms a current pupil on its own. Pupils are not asked for a national ID or a passport.

A teacher is invited by the school. Until that invite is accepted, nobody can publish as a teacher.

An alumnus is invited one at a time, enters the year they left, and receives the verified badge only after a teacher or an already verified alumnus confirms they finished at Pestalozzi. Until that badge is on, they cannot publish an answer.

## The feed

There is one feed: **Pestalozzi Academy · Mathematics**.

Every question carries four labels:

| Label | Required | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grade | Yes | Grade 10, Grade 11, Grade 12, Grade 13 |
| Curriculum | Yes | ECZ, IGCSE, A Level, IB Analysis and Approaches SL, IB Analysis and Approaches HL |
| Topic | Yes | Quadratics, trigonometry, indices, calculus |
| Exam paper | No | October/November 2024, Paper 4, question 3 |

Grade, curriculum, and topic are labels, not separate feeds. Similar questions stay in one list. A Grade 11 quadratics question sits near a Grade 12 quadratics question, and an IGCSE one sits near an IB one. Filters narrow the list to a grade, a curriculum, a topic, or all three.

A past-paper question keeps its own thread. The topic label still links it to the other questions on that topic. A pupil who is not asking about a paper leaves the exam-paper fields empty.

A teacher can pin one question for the week. It sits at the top of the feed. That pin is the weekly challenge: pupils, teachers, and alumni can all answer it.

## Votes, reactions, and comments

Pupils, teachers, and alumni upvote and downvote questions and answers.

- The answer score sorts the replies under a question.
- The pupil who asked can mark one answer as accepted. A teacher can mark one answer on the weekly challenge as the pick.
- A question that falls to **−5** leaves the feed. The author is told it was hidden because of the score.
- A downvote changes the score only after the voter has reached **Trusted** (100 lifetime points), or the voter is a teacher. Until then the vote is saved and shown, and it does not move the score.

Reactions sit beside the vote and do not change the score:

| Reaction | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Helpful | Author gets 1 point, up to 5 a day |
| Brilliant | Shown on the answer. No points |
| Funny | Shown on the answer. No points |

Funny and Brilliant keep the thread lively without letting a joke outrank a correct answer.

Anyone confirmed can leave a short comment under a question or an answer. Comments can be upvoted. A comment that falls to **−5** is hidden.

## Points

Points are earned. They are not bought. Posting alone does not pay. A question or answer earns when someone else finds it useful.

| Action | Who receives them | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ask a question with grade, curriculum, and topic filled in | Asker | 5 |
| Someone upvotes your question | Asker | 2 |
| Accept an answer | Asker | 5 |
| Post an answer | Answerer | 5 |
| Someone upvotes your answer | Answerer | 5 |
| Your answer is accepted | Answerer | 15 |
| A teacher picks your answer on the weekly challenge | Answerer | 15 |
| Your answer is the first on a question that had none | Answerer | 5 extra |
| Vote on a question or answer | Voter | 1, up to 5 points a day |
| Someone marks your post Helpful | Author | 1, up to 5 points a day |

A hidden question (score −5) loses the points it had earned. An answer that falls to **−3** costs the answerer 10 points.

Teachers earn points the same way when they answer. They are hosts of the board, not racers on it.

Lifetime points decide the level:

| Points | Level |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | New |
| 25 | Regular |
| 100 | Trusted |
| 300 | Guide |
| 700 | Pillar |

Trusted is also the line where a pupil or alumnus downvote starts to move the score.

## Streaks

A streak is kept by a useful action: asking, answering, voting, commenting, or accepting. Opening the site does not count.

**Pupils** run a school-day streak, Monday to Friday. One useful action on a weekday continues it. Saturday and Sunday stay off the clock: they do not continue the streak and they do not break it. Every time the streak reaches 5 school days, the pupil earns one freeze, stored up to two. A freeze covers one missed weekday. Each 5 school days of the current streak also pays **10 points**.

**Teachers and alumni** run a week streak. A teacher’s week counts when they pin a challenge or post an answer. An alumnus’s week counts when they post at least one answer. A missed week breaks it. A freeze is earned after a 4-week streak and covers one missed week. The same 10-point bonus is paid every 4 weeks of the current streak.

## Boards and badges

The weekly board shows points earned that week on the Mathematics feed, and it resets on Monday. Pupils and alumni are listed separately, ten places each. The teacher who pinned the challenge is named above the board as the host. Lifetime points stay on the profile.

Badges sit on the profile next to the level:

- First accepted answer
- 5-day pupil streak, and a 4-week teacher or alumnus streak
- Five accepted answers in one topic
- First answer on a past-paper question
- Weekly challenge pick

The teacher badge and the verified alumnus badge are separate from this list. One means the person teaches at Pestalozzi. The other means they finished there.
